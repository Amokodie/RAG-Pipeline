# D04 — Service Bulletin SB-17: Hot-Day Heavy-Payload Thermal Control
Document type: Service bulletin  
Status: Active  
Effective date: 2026-02-20

Field testing showed elevated thermal risk when the aircraft climbs continuously in hot weather with heavy payload.

Operational rule:
- If **ambient temperature > 34 °C** and **payload > 7 kg**, reduce continuous climb power from **92%** to **85%**.

In-flight action:
- If **battery core temperature reaches 50 °C**, initiate a **cooling hold pattern** and **return to base**.
- The cooling hold pattern is a low-climb, low-turn maneuver intended to reduce thermal load while maintaining control authority.

This bulletin does not replace the maintenance manual. It adds an operational safeguard for a specific condition.
