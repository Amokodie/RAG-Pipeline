# D06 — Troubleshooting Guide for Cooling Faults
Document type: Troubleshooting guide  
Status: Reference  
Effective date: 2026-01-18

Symptom 1: **Cooling fan asymmetry**
Definition: the left-right fan electrical draw becomes uneven beyond the maintenance threshold.

Likely causes:
- Blade fouling
- Loose fan connector
- Fan controller drift

First action:
- **Inspect the connector before replacing the fan**

Symptom 2: **Uneven thermal map**
Definition: **Pack-ΔT > 8 °C**

Likely causes:
- Blocked local airflow
- Poor phase-change pad contact
- Local cell degradation

Technician note:
Users may describe cooling fan asymmetry with different words such as:
- uneven fan draw
- left-right fan mismatch
- fan current imbalance
