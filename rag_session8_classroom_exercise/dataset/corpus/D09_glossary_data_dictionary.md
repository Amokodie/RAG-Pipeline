# D09 — Glossary and Data Dictionary
Document type: Glossary  
Status: Reference  
Effective date: 2026-01-10

Definitions:
- **Thermal runaway**: self-accelerating exothermic failure of a battery cell or pack
- **Cell venting precursor**: warning stage before full runaway; may include gas release, odor, local overheating, or rapidly growing thermal imbalance
- **Fan current imbalance**: absolute difference between left and right fan current
- **Cooling hold pattern**: temporary low-stress flight profile used to reduce thermal load
- **Flight cycle**: one takeoff-to-landing mission sequence
- **Pack-ΔT**: difference between hottest and coolest measured pack regions

Language note:
In field reports, technicians may use different phrases for the same signal pattern.  
For example, “uneven fan draw” may refer to fan current imbalance, and “hot spot spread” may refer to Pack-ΔT.
