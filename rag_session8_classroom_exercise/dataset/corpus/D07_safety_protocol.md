# D07 — Emergency Safety Protocol for Charging-Rack Thermal Events
Document type: Safety protocol  
Status: Current  
Effective date: 2026-01-12

If smoke, solvent odor, or visible vapor appears during charging:
1. **Isolate the charging rack**
2. **Disconnect the external charger**
3. Move the pack to the **sand-lined containment cart**
4. Notify the duty supervisor
5. Record the time, temperature, and operator observations

Do not spray water directly into an opened battery pack enclosure.  
Water may be used only on surrounding materials if required by site fire procedure and if the battery pack itself is already isolated.
